How to use

  1. Choose a yarn from the list at the top. Your colour choices are kept for each yarn, so you can switch brands and come back without losing them.
  2. Tap A, B, C or D on a pair, then tap a colour to try it on the slippers.
  3. Type the grams for each colour from your pattern.
  4. Add more pairs if you're knitting several. The shopping list adds up all pairs, so shared colours need fewer balls.
Colours on screen are approximations from shop photos. Your choices are saved in this browser only.
